Sound familiar?

Most businesses grow faster
than their systems do.

When a business scales without structure, the founder becomes the system. Everything routes through one person because nothing else exists to hold it.

You don't have a reliable picture of your financial position
Revenue is visible. Profit is a guess. Cash position requires work to calculate. When someone asks how the business is doing, the honest answer is you're not entirely sure because the data hasn't been organised.
Important things keep slipping through
Not because you're not across it, but because the business doesn't have the capacity. Follow-ups don't happen. Reconciliations are weeks behind. Things that should have been done last month are still waiting.
You are the only person who knows how things get done
Decisions, approvals, and context all come back to you. When you're unavailable, work stops. Nothing is written down. Every new person needs a full walkthrough you don't have time to give.
You're doing too many jobs at once
Finance, operations, communication, execution — you're carrying more than one person should because handing things off requires structure that doesn't exist yet. Growth is making you more of a bottleneck, not less.

These problems don't go away by working harder. They go away with better structure, cleaner financial information, and someone you can trust to follow through — which is exactly what I provide.

Are you looking for employment or offering freelance services?
Both, and I'm clear about the difference. I'm open to permanent full-time remote employment and available for project-based or monthly retainer engagements. The skills are the same either way — the arrangement is just different. If you're a recruiter or employer, the About page has my full background and credentials. If you're a founder or business owner looking for operational support, the Services page covers scope and structure.
How is this different from hiring a virtual assistant?
A VA does what you tell them, when you tell them. I build systems, anticipate needs, and manage operations proactively. My background in Economics, Mathematics, and financial analysis means I understand the business impact of each decision — not just how to execute the task. The difference is a task taker versus a professional who understands the full picture.
Do you have remote work experience?
Yes. At Pitch Investors I supported a CEO for nearly a year, handling coordination, investor communication, and financial deliverables remotely — with the CEO based in the US. I operate both synchronously and asynchronously by default, and communicate proactively rather than waiting to be checked on.
What does a typical engagement look like?
We start with a free 30-minute discovery call. If there's a fit, I recommend either a project engagement or a retainer depending on your situation. The first week is a deep-dive audit of your books, workflows, and systems. By week two, you're seeing results. Project engagements close with full documentation. Retainers continue monthly.
Do you work with clients outside Kenya?
Yes. I work remotely with clients and employers worldwide and operate across time zones both synchronously and asynchronously. Most communication happens via Slack or email, with scheduled weekly sync calls where needed.
What tools do you work with?
QuickBooks, Xero, Zoho Books, Excel, Power BI, Google Workspace, Notion, Zapier, Airtable, ClickUp, Trello, Asana, Slack, Zoom, and others. If you use something not on this list, I pick it up quickly. The goal is to reduce friction, not add it.
How do you handle confidential financial data?
Confidentiality is standard. I sign NDAs on request, use your preferred password manager, and follow whatever security protocols you have in place. My background in financial analysis means I'm accustomed to handling sensitive data with care.
Can you replace my accountant or CFO?
No, and I'll be direct about that. I'm not a licensed CPA and don't file statutory accounts or handle tax compliance that requires registration. What I do is the operational and reporting layer: clean books, accurate records, usable dashboards, and financial models. If you have a CFO or accountant, I work alongside them — handling the day-to-day so they can focus on strategy.
