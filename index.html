Executive Operations · Project

CEO Calendar Chaos → Structured System (Maya Patel, Velocity Labs)

The Problem

Maya Patel, Founder & CEO of a 15-person B2B SaaS agency, had 47 meetings scattered across Gmail, Slack, and paper planner. No meeting prep materials, investors frustrated by missed check-ins, back-to-backs with no buffer, and co-founder booking without permission. Three calendar tools in use simultaneously.

What Was Delivered
  • Full calendar audit — meetings categorized by type, time allocation analyzed
  • New calendar architecture with color-coded system, protected focus blocks
  • Scheduling workflow built — Google Form for meeting requests
  • Standardized 1-on-1 agenda template for all 5 direct reports
  • Migrated from 3 tools to Google Calendar as single source of truth
Google Calendar Google Forms Notion Google Sheets
Verified Outcomes
Results
47 meetings → system 3 tools → 1 Focus time protected Investor calls tracked

Maya moved from reactive scheduling to intentional time ownership. The system runs without daily management. Investor check-ins no longer missed.
