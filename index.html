Sound familiar?

These aren't your fault.
They're predictable at this stage.

When a business grows faster than its systems, the founder becomes the system. Everything routes through you — not because you want it to, but because nothing else exists to hold it.

You don't know exactly how the business is doing financially
Revenue is visible. Profit is a guess. Cash position requires work to figure out. When someone asks how the business is going, the honest answer is you're not sure — not because the data isn't there, but because nobody's organised it.
Important things keep slipping through the cracks
Not because you're not on top of it — because the business doesn't have the capacity. Follow-ups don't happen. Reconciliations are weeks behind. Things that should have been done last month are still waiting because something more urgent always comes up first.
You're the only one who knows how things actually get done
Decisions, approvals, context — they all come back to you. When you're not available, work stops. Nothing is written down. Every new person on the team needs a full walkthrough you don't have time to give.
You're doing too many jobs at once
Finance, operations, communication, execution — you're carrying more than one person should. Not because you haven't tried to hand things off, but because handing things off requires structure that doesn't exist yet.

These problems don't go away by working harder. They go away with better structure, clearer financial information, and someone you trust to follow through — which is what I provide.
