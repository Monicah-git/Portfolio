Cutting payment discrepancies by 60% through tighter AP/AR controls

+
The problem

Late payments were stacking up. Petty cash did not balance. Invoicing was slow, which meant cash collection was slow. The business had the revenue. It just could not track or collect it efficiently.

What I did
  • Overhauled the AP/AR tracking system with structured follow-up workflows
  • Implemented daily petty cash reconciliation with proper documentation and approval controls
  • Refined the invoicing process to reduce turnaround from issue to payment confirmation
  • Streamlined payroll processing while maintaining 100% on-time disbursements

Results
Outcomes
