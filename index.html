Founders outgrow their systems
before anyone notices.

When a business grows without structure, the founder ends up filling the gap. Work routes through one person because there are no reliable systems behind it.

Your financial picture is blurry
Revenue is visible. Profit is a guess. Cash position requires manual work to calculate. When someone asks how the business is doing, the honest answer is you're not entirely sure, because your financial data hasn't been organised into something useful like reports.
Repetitive work still requires a person every time
Invoice follow ups, report assembly, expense tracking, renewal chasing the same steps done manually, over and over. The work isn't complex. It's just never been set up to run itself.
How things get done lives in people's heads
Processes are not documented. Every new hire needs a personal walkthrough. When someone leaves, knowledge goes with them. When you are unavailable, work stops, because nothing is written down.
Your time goes to things that shouldn't reach you
Scheduling, inbox management, follow ups, status checks. Work that routes back to the founder because no one else has the access, context, or authority to handle it. Every one of those is a distraction from the actual job.

These problems share a root cause: no one owns the operational and financial layer. That's the gap I fill, working directly with the founder to build the structure, run the numbers, and keep things moving.

What is a Finance and Operations Partner?
A Finance and Operations Partner takes ownership of the full operational and financial layer of a business. That means accurate financial records, reporting that surfaces useful information, automated workflows that remove repetitive manual work, documented processes so the business doesn't depend on one person's memory, and direct executive support for the CEO. The role reports directly to the founder not through an operations director or team lead. One person, accountable for the whole layer.
How is this different from hiring a bookkeeper, a coordinator, or a virtual assistant?
A bookkeeper maintains records and stops there. A coordinator manages tasks and stops there. A Finance and Operations Partner works across all three areas, financial operations, systems and automation and executive support and owns the outcomes, not just the tasks. My background in Economics, Mathematics, and Financial Engineering means I understand what the numbers imply, not just how to record them. When I see a discrepancy, I investigate why. When I build a process, I design it to run without me. The difference is ownership versus execution.
Are you looking for employment or offering consulting services?
Both, and I am clear about the difference. I am open to permanent full-time remote employment in finance, operations, or executive support roles. I am also available for project based and monthly retainer consulting engagements. The skills and approach are the same either way. If you are a recruiter or employer, the About page has my full background and credentials.
Do you have remote work experience?
Yes. At Pitch Investors I supported a US-based CEO for 8 months remotely delivering financial packages, managing investor communication and coordinating operations across time zones. I operate asynchronously by default, communicate proactively and produce finished work without needing to be checked on.
What does a typical engagement look like?
We start with a free 30 minute call. If there is a fit, I recommend a project or retainer depending on your situation. Week one is a review of your books, workflows, tools and calendar I ensure gaps are identified and priorities are confirmed. Work starts against the agreed scope. Project engagements close with full documentation. Retainers continue monthly with a weekly sync.
What does this cost?
Scope and investment are discussed on the discovery call, once I understand what actually needs doing. Projects are priced on deliverables and timeline which is confirmed before work begins. Retainers are based on monthly scope agreed at onboarding, billed on actuals. No surprises on either side.
Do you work with clients outside Kenya?
Yes. I work remotely with founders and employers worldwide. Most communication happens via Slack or email, with a weekly sync call. I operate across time zones both synchronously and asynchronously.
What tools do you work with?
QuickBooks, Xero, Zoho Books, Excel, Google Sheets, Power BI, Notion, Zapier, Make, Airtable, ClickUp, Trello, Asana, Slack, Zoom, Google Workspace, and others. If you use something not on this list, I pick it up quickly.
Can you replace my accountant or CFO?
No, I am direct about that. I am not a licensed CPA and do not file statutory accounts or handle tax compliance requiring registration. What I do is the financial operations layer preparing accurate records, useful reporting, documenting workflows and direct executive support. If you have a CFO or accountant, I work alongside them and handle the day to day so they focus on strategy.
