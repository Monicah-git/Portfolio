Executive Operations · Simulated Project

CEO Calendar Chaos → Structured System

47 meetings → 1 system3 tools → 1 calendarFocus time protected daily5 SOPs live
The situation

Maya Patel, Founder and CEO of a 15-person B2B SaaS agency, had 47 meetings scattered across Gmail, Slack, and a paper planner. No prep materials, investors frustrated by missed check-ins, back-to-backs with no breathing room, and her co-founder booking her without any approval process. Three calendar tools running simultaneously with nothing connecting them.

What was delivered
  • Full calendar audit — meetings categorised by type, time allocation mapped
  • New architecture with colour-coded system, protected focus blocks, and clear scheduling rules
  • Scheduling workflow built — Google Form for requests, authority levels defined
  • Standardised 1-on-1 agenda template for all 5 direct reports
  • Migrated from 3 tools to Google Calendar as the single source of truth

Results
